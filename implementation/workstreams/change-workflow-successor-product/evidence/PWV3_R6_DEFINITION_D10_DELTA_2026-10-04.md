2. Final Qualification.
   - The universal external Targeted Bug Hunt OP boundary is removed.

3. Global disposition.
   - Applicable Global CLEAR advances deterministically to fresh terminal acceptance without the ordinary CONTINUE / RUN OP AGAIN owner gate.

   - A changed exact candidate or materially relevant acceptance surface requires a new applicable independent Global result.

   - Fresh terminal acceptance remains a PWV3 semantic predicate.
   - A qualified semantically independent read-only non-authoring reviewer may execute it automatically after applicable Global CLEAR.
   - Runtime/provider self-review or a material author/repairer cannot satisfy semantic independence.

5. Initial 3.0 scope.
   - All previously planned later-3.x compatibility/evolution capabilities move into initial 3.0 as concrete capability or complete enabling framework/harness.

   - Concrete readers/adapters for future schemas naturally arrive with those future schemas.

6. Construction handoff.
   - PWV2 governs only through accepted Definition, Planning/Plan Review, Execution Prep and accepted Execution Package Review.
   - PWV2 does not execute the accepted package.
   - The exact reviewed package is handed to an external qualified execution environment which performs implementation outside PWV2 governance.

   - Self-hosting remains later candidate qualification/dogfood, not a mid-implementation PWV2 -> partial-PWV3 authority cut.
