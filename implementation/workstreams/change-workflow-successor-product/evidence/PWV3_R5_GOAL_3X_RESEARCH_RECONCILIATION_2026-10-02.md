- Planning remains execution-provider-neutral.

### Two execution realizations

- `native_pwv3`;
- `goal`.

Goal is a late-bound execution realization, not a second workflow.

### Goal provider and mandatory subagents

- No provider is selected by name in semantic authority.

### Goal-owned delivery and Final Qualification

Goal mode may autonomously own, under the exact accepted Execution Package:
- implementation;

- bounded implementation-only repair;
- ordinary Review/revalidation;
- non-independent Targeted Qualification Campaign;
- other non-independent Final Qualification;
- exact candidate freeze and readiness proof.

It must then stop at:
`AWAITING_EXTERNAL_GLOBAL_BUG_HUNT`.

### Global Bug Hunt

- The independent Global Bug Hunt remains outside goal execution in ChatGPT/OP.
- It is the normal manual independent bug-hunt boundary.
- CLEAR routes deterministically to fresh terminal closure acceptance without a routine second owner CONTINUE gate.
- Implementation-only findings return to the already-selected execution mode under the same accepted Execution Package plus the exact finding set.
- If repair changes the exact candidate or relevant acceptance surface, invalidated checks/reviews/targeted qualification repeat and a new applicable independent Global Bug Hunt is required.
- Findings that alter Execution Package, Plan, Definition or owner authority route to the earliest semantic owner.
- BLOCKED/UNKNOWN fails closed.

### Terminal acceptance

Fresh terminal acceptance remains a PWV3 semantic closure predicate, not a provider completion bit.

A qualified semantically independent, read-only, non-authoring closure reviewer may execute that predicate automatically inside the same top-level goal lineage. Provider self-review cannot substitute.
