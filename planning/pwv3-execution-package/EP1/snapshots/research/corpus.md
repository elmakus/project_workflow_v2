# 5. Disposition semantics

The final corpus uses these dispositions consistently.

## V3_3_0_REGRESSION_REQUIRED

The invariant is required by the accepted 3.0 safety spine or Final Qualification. The exact historical representation may be removed, but a native PWV3 scenario must prove the invariant.

## V3_LATER_REGRESSION

The invariant belongs only to a feature intentionally outside 3.0. It must not be used to defer a 3.0 safety property.

## V3_IMPLEMENTATION_GUARD_REQUIRED

The result is primarily a structural/trust/architecture constraint. It should have compact negative/conformance tests where practical, but it is not necessarily a standalone end-user scenario.

## MIGRATE_AS_V3_ISSUE_ONLY_IF_REPRODUCED

Historical evidence becomes a current V3 defect only after an applicable exact PWV3 subject reproduces or structurally proves the failure.

This rule is globally applicable to historical defect provenance. It is not permission to pre-create trackers.

## HISTORICAL_REFERENCE_ONLY

Useful chronology, prior art, withdrawn evidence, bookkeeping or removed implementation history that does not itself become a PWV3 regression.

## DROP_V2_ONLY_TRIGGER

The literal old mechanism is intentionally absent in PWV3. Its surviving invariant, if any, is represented elsewhere.

## Admission, routing and transfer

### PWV3-REG-ADMISSION-FRESHNESS-001

Before route derivation, prove all required freshness domains independently:

- authoritative consumer/workstream ref;
- compatible immutable PWV3 package/policy/helper identity;
- required pointed immutable objects.

Required negatives include stale local checkout, detached/wrong checkout, local SHA different from authoritative remote, stale package with fresh consumer, fresh package with stale consumer, missing pointed object, ref moving after initial fetch, and unavailable network/auth/freshness proof.

Unprovable freshness is UNKNOWN/BLOCKED, never cached GREEN.

Provenance: overflow R100, runtime interchangeability research, F1/F12.

### PWV3-REG-ROUTE-OWNER-CLOSURE-001

A materialized higher/current owner or prerequisite cannot be bypassed by a lower runnable stage. Individually parse-valid but relationally contradictory state fails closed.

Provenance: #32, #36, historical router-board precedence.

### PWV3-REG-STOP-PRECEDENCE-001

A real explicit user/authorization/independence/blocker/end boundary outranks downstream runnable state. Conversely RED/GREEN/role completion is not a stop by itself.

Provenance: #11, #16, #33.

### PWV3-REG-RESEARCH-RETURN-001

Completed Research returns exactly once to its exact recorded owner. Crash before reconciliation resumes idempotently; replay cannot double-consume; wrong owner fails closed; alternate projections cannot shadow the return.

Provenance: #28, NI-12.

### PWV3-REG-CONT-NONSTOP-001

After durable non-stop outcome, reread canonical truth and derive/continue the next already-authorized obligation. Runtime/model/worker response completion does not terminate the semantic workflow.

Provenance: #16.

### PWV3-REG-HANDOFF-BOUNDARY-001

One parameterized transfer contract must prove:

- required handoff is observably delivered at a real boundary;
- non-boundary transition does not fabricate a handoff;
- receiver treats the envelope as untrusted bootstrap and reconciles canonical state;
- exact valid current transfer is consumed once without redundant generic confirmation or identical handoff replay;
- stale/wrong/forged/malformed/duplicate handoff fails closed;
- required independence is semantic/material, not session/profile identity.

Provenance: #11, #17, #25.

## Planning and Review

### PWV3-REG-PLAN-GRAPH-001

Compose execution/material-input dependencies with acceptance/review gates. Reject a logical cycle where downstream work is needed to create acceptance required before that downstream work may consume its predecessor.

No parallel scheduler is required to express the case.

Provenance: #14.

### PWV3-REG-PLANREVIEW-NONTERM-001

Every supported Plan Review state has explicit semantics. pending/in_progress remains nonterminal or unsupported/fail-closed; no catch-all maps it to terminal RED/GREEN.

Provenance: #40.

### PWV3-REG-REVIEW-SUBJECT-ACCEPT-001

GREEN binds both exact immutable reviewed subject and exact immutable/applicable acceptance surface. Change either independently and prove old GREEN cannot authorize the changed surface.

Provenance: #30, #26.

### PWV3-REG-REVIEW-HISTORY-001

Terminal Review/Plan Review attempt is immutable history. Verdict/subject/evidence change creates a new attempt. An invalid historical GREEN remains evidence but is unusable; a lawful successor attempt may become current without rewriting history.

Provenance: #18, #31, #37.

### PWV3-REG-REVIEW-INDEP-001

A context that materially authored/repaired the exact subject cannot independently approve it. A fresh instance of the same model/profile may be eligible if it did not materially author/repair the subject. Runtime/profile names never prove independence.

Provenance: architecture independence contract, #18 family, runtime lanes.

## Execution, Result and evidence

### PWV3-REG-EXEC-RESUME-001

Fresh resume of an active Card revalidates its stable accepted contract, current authority and exact accepted dependency Results before Execution. Status alone cannot waive admission checks.

Provenance: #35.

### PWV3-REG-EXEC-ONE-CARD-001

Exactly one ordinary Project Card is semantically active/executing. A state attempting two active ordinary Cards fails closed or deterministically identifies the single legal Card. Runtime-internal parallelism under one Card is non-authoritative realization only.

Provenance: accepted architecture, removed scheduler evidence.

### PWV3-REG-RESULT-SUBJECT-001

Result implementation subject must be a permitted exact immutable implementation identity. Arbitrary prose, branch-only locator, malformed or nonexistent identity cannot grant no-replay.

Provenance: #39.

### PWV3-REG-RESULT-OBJECT-001

Resolve repository/commit/path/blob or equivalent immutable subject to the actual bytes consumed. Nonexistent objects, stale coordinates, same-path changed bytes and dirty working-tree substitutions fail closed.

At least one release test uses a real disposable Git repository, not only 40-hex-shaped fixtures.

Provenance: #34, NI-03.

### PWV3-REG-EVIDENCE-PRESENCE-001

All required Result/Review/qualification evidence locators resolve to durable evidence bound to the exact subject. Deleting or changing required evidence blocks acceptance/terminality.

Provenance: #38.

### PWV3-REG-RESULT-NOREPLAY-RELEASE-001

A valid durable Result prevents implementation replay after runtime loss. If Review is required, that same Result does not yet release downstream dependency/terminal completion until applicable Review/finalization is satisfied.

Provenance: architecture/bridge Result boundary, Execution lane.

### PWV3-REG-TERMINAL-PROOF-001

DONE/complete/close-ready status cannot substitute for exact current Result, required evidence, required applicable Review and other acceptance proof. Close remains unreachable until semantic closure is reconstructible.

Provenance: #27.

## External observation and external effects

### PWV3-REG-OBS-PENDING-001

For exact-subject external/CI evidence:

- queued/requested/waiting/pending/in_progress is unresolved;
- terminal positive may satisfy the gate;
- terminal negative/cancelled/timed_out/action_required is negative evidence;
- missing just-triggered observation requires bounded readback;
- wrong-subject/malformed observation fails closed.

Agent speed cannot turn pending into success.

Provenance: #22.

### PWV3-REG-EFFECT-UNKNOWN-001

Material external effect follows intent -> attempt -> authoritative readback -> verified outcome.

UNKNOWN/may-have-succeeded never authorizes blind retry. Verified no-effect may authorize retry; verified expected effect reconciles without replay; verified unexpected effect requires reconciliation.

Provenance: bridge/architecture external-effect evidence, NI-13.

### PWV3-REG-EFFECT-IDEMPOTENCY-001

Where provider-native idempotency exists, one token binds one exact caller intent. Retry of the same intent reuses the token; a distinct legitimate intent with identical parameters receives a new token; changed intent cannot inherit the old token. Secrets are not persisted as workflow state.

Provenance: architecture/effects lane.

## Publication, remote precedence and round-trip closure

### PWV3-REG-PUB-WRITER-READER-001

Use the actual canonical writer path to produce an authoritative PWV3 artifact, validate before publication, publish, positively read back exact remote bytes, then feed those bytes to the same canonical fresh consumer/derive-next path.

A fixture-only serializer/parser pair is insufficient.

Provenance: #23, #26, red-team producer/consumer warning.

### PWV3-REG-PUB-CAS-CRASH-001

Cover both:

- stale writer: two attempts start from old remote O; after A publishes successor, B's write still expecting O must fail;
- crash windows: before ref update means no canonical effect; after successful mutation but before acknowledgement/readback may already have effect and must reconcile from remote truth.

No force/last-writer-wins authority.

Provenance: NI-01, NI-02.

### PWV3-REG-REMOTE-PRECEDENCE-001

Parameterize exact base, safe unpublished local descendant with unchanged remote, remote fast-forward, remote-ahead divergent local WIP, dirty local bytes and ambiguous/rewritten ancestry.

Remote durable truth wins on divergence. Stale local semantic work is not silently auto-merged/rebased/cherry-picked as authority. Ambiguity enters Recovery.

Provenance: Recovery architecture and runtime audits.

### PWV3-REG-INTEGRATION-DRIFT-001

Freeze exact source H, target/base B0, candidate/tree T and acceptance A. Then cover:

- source head changes;
- target moves in provably unrelated surface;
- target moves materially;
- material impact cannot be bounded;
- target moves after final refresh before mutation.

Reuse is allowed only after recomputing the integration candidate and proving bounded non-impact plus required GREEN evidence. Material/unknown change creates revalidation/new subject.

Provenance: Git/Close lane, NI-16.

### PWV3-REG-MERGE-READBACK-001

Merge is an external effect. A request response or timeout is not final truth.

Read back exact PR, expected source head/target, required checks and target integration. If expected merge occurred, reconcile without retry; if verified no effect, retry only after freshness revalidation; wrong/unqualified/unexpected integration requires reconciliation.

Provenance: architecture/Close research.

### PWV3-REG-CLOSE-RESTART-001

After verified accepted merge/Close:

- source branch may be auto-deleted;
- target may later advance with unrelated commits;
- runtime/session/cache may disappear.

Fresh entry reconstructs terminal end-of-scope from target-side canonical package plus immutable PR/merge evidence. Missing unique recovery artifact fails closed. Source branch survival and optional cleanup are non-semantic.

Provenance: #29, historical M07 Close evidence.

## Evolution and revalidation

### PWV3-REG-VERSION-BOUNDARY-001

From the first release:

- every durable artifact kind declares a producing PWV3 contract version;
- current writer/current reader parity is proven;
- unknown/newer required contract fails closed before mutation;
- helper/package upgrade alone does not stale unchanged semantic evidence;
- historical bytes are never silently rewritten merely because runtime upgraded.

The 3.0 implementation need not ship a production N-1 reader before a real earlier PWV3 contract exists. It must ship the explicit version boundary and fail-closed behavior needed to add one lawfully.

Provenance: #23, evolution lane, roadmap reconciliation.

### PWV3-REG-IMPACT-REVALIDATION-001

Given changed Result/dependency/acceptance/target/current rule:

- preserve unaffected evidence only when non-impact is proven;
- revalidate materially affected uses;
- changed implementation produces a new Result;
- uncertainty widens revalidation rather than guessing narrowly;
- stronger current rule may require new evidence while historical bytes remain immutable.

Provenance: evolution lane, #30, NI-11.

### PWV3-REG-CLEAN-LINEAGE-001

Native PWV3 startup does not interpret V1/V2/V2.2 lifecycle state as PWV3 historical state. No missing version is guessed and no field alias silently converts legacy state.

If a one-off legacy import tool is ever authorized, it remains external extract -> validate -> initialize and ordinary PWV3 routing has no runtime dependency on it.

Provenance: architecture clean-lineage decision, bridge/donor strategy.

### PWV3-REG-REPAIR-CAUSAL-001

Provenance: NI-08, owner-fixed OP direction, Final Qualification architecture.

Any later material candidate or acceptance change invalidates old final GREEN and requires current applicability/revalidation. The candidate is never its sole oracle for qualification or promotion.

Provenance: architecture/roadmap Final Qualification.

# 11. Rule for creating a new PWV3 Issue from historical evidence

Default: do not migrate.

A new elmakus/pwv3 Issue may be created only when every gate below is satisfied.

## Gate A — valid provenance

The historical observation has reliable chronology and exact evidence.

Withdrawn, superseded or ambiguous history may seed a regression idea but cannot establish a current defect.

Issue #15 is the canonical negative example.

## Gate B — invariant applies to current PWV3

Ask separately:

1. Does the old trigger still exist?
2. Does the underlying invariant still exist?

If the trigger is removed but the invariant survives, keep a regression/guard. Do not create a V3 Issue merely because the old Issue was severe or still open.

## Gate C — exact current PWV3 reproduction or structural proof

Bind the failure to an immutable PWV3 commit/release/artifact/candidate and current contract.

Acceptable evidence:

- deterministic failing V3-native regression;
- supported-host live reproduction with authoritative readback;
- exact structural validation proving a mandatory current V3 guard is violated.

Not acceptable:

- V2 Issue is open;
- similar code exists;
- a V2 test once failed;
- a model predicts recurrence;
- stale screenshots/logs with unresolved chronology;
- a V2 candidate branch contains a repair.

Before implementation exists, historical findings are corpus requirements, not V3 defects.

## Gate D — deduplicate by current root cause

Search current V3 tracker/current candidate state.

Create one Issue per current V3 root repair obligation, not one per V2 number. Several historical Issues may collapse into one V3 defect; one historical Issue may split if distinct V3 invariants reproduce independently.

## Gate E — write the Issue in V3 terms

Record:

- exact V3 subject;
- exact reproduction;
- violated V3 invariant/acceptance;
- expected vs actual;
- affected host/surface;
- durable evidence/readback;
- regression ID;
- dedup result.

Historical V2 IDs are provenance only.

## Gate F — Issue state remains bookkeeping

Creating/closing the Issue does not replace Definition/Plan/Result/Review authority and does not manufacture approval.

If the accepted PWV3 health predicate treats a confirmed current defect as BLOCKED, that remains a narrow product rule over verified tracker evidence, not a second workflow state machine.

This whole policy is MIGRATE_AS_V3_ISSUE_ONLY_IF_REPRODUCED.

# 16. Final integrated conclusion

The defect-memory bridge from PWV2/V2.2 to PWV3 is not a compatibility layer and not a migrated tracker.

It is a deliberately smaller but stricter native PWV3 assurance corpus that preserves the failure invariants most likely to create false GREEN:

- stale or contradictory authority;
- unresolved object/evidence identity;
- stale subject/acceptance Review reuse;
- mutable Review history;
- impossible Plan legality;
- Research/continuation/handoff replay;
- active-Card authority bypass;
- Result replay or premature dependency release;
- pending external evidence treated as success;
- UNKNOWN effects retried blindly;
- stale publication and post-write crash ambiguity;
- local state outranking remote truth;
- target/source drift around final integration;
- Close that cannot reconstruct after source/session loss;
- version/package/runtime changes treated as semantic authority;
- child/worker completion treated as workflow completion;
- incomplete discovery or partial/root-cause-incomplete repair;
- stale receiver snapshots that are internally valid but globally non-authoritative.

PWV3 3.0 should encode these as invariant-based deterministic regressions plus live supported-host/provider qualification. V2 Issue IDs remain provenance only. Removed V2 machinery stays removed. A new V3 Issue exists only when an applicable defect is reproduced against an exact current PWV3 subject.

That is the minimum defect/regression corpus that preserves the predecessor's hard-earned failure memory without preserving the predecessor itself.
