## OP abstraction and runtime contract

PWV3 selects **whether** an exact semantic boundary requires OP. Orchestration Protocol selects **how** that wave is executed.

When the current runtime is ChatGPT:
- the owner-facing/coordinator ChatGPT may remain the same Main across Brainstorming, Research orchestration, Definition and other legal work;
- PWV3 invokes the current compatible Orchestration Protocol skill/protocol for an OP-selected boundary;
- the OP protocol, not PWV3, owns worker topology, lane isolation, fresh-context launchers, coverage, integration mechanics and focused post-repair revalidation;
- ordinary same-context stage transitions do not create ceremonial handoffs.

When Pi/Paseo reaches an enabled OP boundary:
- Pi/Paseo must not substitute child agents, subagents or Generic Workers for OP;
- it emits the runtime-neutral handoff to ChatGPT and stops at that assurance boundary;
- ChatGPT executes OP and publishes the durable integrated result;
- canonical PWV3 routing then resumes on whichever runtime is legally selected next.

Fresh ChatGPT worker/reviewer/integrator contexts required by OP are OP realization details. They do not replace the owner-facing coordinator chat and they are not canonical workflow identity.

OP discovery is not fail-fast: it completes bounded declared coverage/convergence after findings rather than stopping at the first blocker. Integration occurs before ordinary repair. Plan Review and Execution Package Review use one full OP discovery wave per frozen subject/cycle, followed after bounded repair by fresh focused independent revalidation. A second full OP wave is required only after material scope/strategy/acceptance/review-surface change, unbounded impact, failed applicability or a materially new defect class invalidating the original coverage.
