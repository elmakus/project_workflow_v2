## Common evidence contract

S14–S16 are sequential non-Card obligations after all thirteen implementation Cards are accepted. They have no mutable Task Board status and no hidden backwards prerequisite for early Card acceptance. Coverage test paths carrying an S14–S16 suffix identify the gate's runnable facet, not a new code-authoring Card. Their runners/oracles are implemented under the earlier component Card owning that surface (S08 provider/Close, S10 OP, S11/S12 host, S13 integrated/terminal); qualification runs and records them, and routes needed code/oracle changes back to those stable Cards. Exact inputs are accepted immutable Results/Reviews plus their actual implementation/acceptance subjects, not just slot labels. Evidence belongs under `construction/qualification/<obligation>/<unique>/` in the recipient and is append-only; currentness must reconstruct from exact accepted package/frontier/evidence, not session labels.

Every evidence cell binds repository, immutable candidate payload, current acceptance, package/helper/artifact versions, observed host/capability bundle, operation, exact action/readback subjects, outcome, test type and limitations. Capture commands and independently verifiable outputs without secrets. Missing, skipped, denied, fixture-only or UNKNOWN is not GREEN. An actual live test is explicitly distinguished from metadata, synthetic tests and disposable Git tests. Guarded real-inference environment policy applies to all live tests that intentionally invoke an LLM; unavailable prescribed profile blocks, with no substitute.

All P1 section-8.4 live rows are required. `COVERAGE.json` identifies their exact local source and contract owner, and the complete corpus snapshot supplies original oracles. D12 supersedes older corpus recommendations: mandatory five OP boundaries, internal non-independent Targeted, no routine post-Global gate, full evolution/fingerprints in 3.0, no execution mode, native pre-QF freshness/waiver semantics, no mid-build SH0 authority transfer.

The causal repair loop is common to every gate. Attribute implementation/oracle defects to the materially affected completed stable Cards plus dependency/acceptance/shared-surface cone. Reopen the earliest affected ID, repair/re-complete in original total order with new Results and all invalidated acceptance. Preserve evidence outside a positively proven cone; widen for UNKNOWN. Then reconstruct the still-outstanding qualification obligation and candidate/surface applicability. Product, Definition, strategy and package meaning changes return upstream; no implementation worker can absorb them. No fixed semantic repeat ceiling and no second repair queue.
